**Why this matters:** The display name is what clients see. Until Meta approves it, clients may see only your phone number.

Check the status in the app: Settings > WhatsApp > Health > Display name. It shows Approved, In review, Declined or Expired.

## Why Meta may decline

Meta says the name must match your business and follow its guidelines. The guidelines cover capital letters and generic names, among others (verify the full list at the link above).

Tips:

- Use your real business name as on your website or documents.
- Do not use only a generic word like "Salon" or "Barber".
- Do not use all capital letters or extra symbols.

## Steps

1. On a computer, open business.facebook.com and sign in.
2. Open WhatsApp Manager.
3. Go to Account tools, then Phone numbers.
4. Select your number.
5. Open the Profile tab.
6. Click Edit next to Display name.
7. Enter the new name and submit.
8. Wait for Meta. Pull down on Settings > WhatsApp to refresh.

You can change the name up to 10 times in 30 days. After approval you have 14 days to re-register the number or you must submit again (verify, support can help with this).

Meta does not state how long a review takes (verify).
