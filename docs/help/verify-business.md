**Why this matters:** A verified business gets higher messaging limits, and Meta can approve your business display name. Without it you start at a low daily limit.

**Do this on a computer.** You upload documents, and that is hard on a phone.

## What it is

Meta checks that your business is real. You send official papers. Meta compares the name and address on them with your Meta business settings.

## What you get

- A higher messaging limit. New accounts start at 250 people per day. Verifying is one way to move up to 2,000.
- Your display name can be shown to clients.
- More room to grow.

## Documents Meta accepts

- Certificate of formation or incorporation.
- Government business license or registration.
- Government tax documents (for example, a US IRS EIN letter).
- Not accepted: utility bills, invoices, or tax forms you filled in yourself with no official stamp.
- The exact list for South Africa: (verify). Meta shows the list for your country during the steps.

The business name and address must match your Meta business settings. The document must not be expired. The scan must be clear.

## Steps

1. On a computer, open business.facebook.com and sign in as the admin.
2. Open Security Center (verify the exact path).
3. Click "Start verification".
4. Confirm your business name, address and phone number.
5. Pick the document type Meta asks for.
6. Upload the document.
7. Pick how Meta confirms you (email, phone or domain) and enter the code.
8. Submit.

If you see "Ineligible for verification", you do not need to do it there.

## How long it takes

Meta says a decision can take up to 14 business days. If Meta asks for more information, answer within one week or you must start again.

## If rejected

Common reasons: name or address does not match, expired document, blurry scan. Fix it and try again. Meta also lets you appeal.

In the app, check Settings > WhatsApp > Business verification. It shows Verified, Pending or Not verified.
