**Why this matters:** Your plan sets how many bookings are included each month. The right size saves money.

For prices, see Settings > Plan. They show in your own currency.

## The plans

- Starter, Growth and Pro.
- Each plan includes a number of bookings per month. Bigger plans include more.
- Every paid plan includes your Clovie companion.

## How bookings are counted

- **Every booking counts.** It does not matter who made it or what happened to it (done, no-show, moved or cancelled).
- **Nothing is cut off.** If you go over, each extra booking costs a small fee, billed at the end of the billing period. The rate is on the Plan screen.
- The app warns you when you reach 80%.
- The count resets on the date shown on the Plan screen.

## Change your plan

Only the owner can do this.

1. Open Settings.
2. Tap Plan.
3. Check "Your plan" and the usage line.
4. In Plans, find the one you want.
5. Tap "Choose" with the plan name.
6. Read the screen that opens. It shows the cost and the date.
7. Tap the confirm button.

## Upgrade or downgrade

- **Upgrade:** happens now. You pay the difference for the rest of this month today. The new price continues from your next billing date. Button: "Pay [amount] and upgrade".
- **Downgrade:** happens at the end of your billing period. You keep your current plan until then. No refund for this month. Button: "Switch on [date]".
- A scheduled downgrade shows a banner: "Switching to [plan] on [date]".
- To cancel it, tap "Keep [plan]".

## If your card is declined

The app says "Your card was declined. Your plan has not changed." Tap "Update card", then try again.

## Card and invoices

In the Billing part of the Plan screen you will see Payment method, Invoices and "Manage subscription". Some of these open on the web. You can cancel anytime.
