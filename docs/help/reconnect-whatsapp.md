**Why this matters:** If the link breaks, Clovie cannot reply to clients or send reminders.

## When to reconnect

- The app says "Reconnect" or "Needs to be reconnected".
- You changed your Facebook or Meta password.
- You lost admin rights in your Meta business.
- Your number was removed from the Meta account.
- Clients say they get no reply.

## Steps

Only the owner can do this.

1. Open Settings.
2. Tap WhatsApp.
3. Check the top row. If it is not "Connected", continue.
4. Tap "Reconnect WhatsApp". If you never connected, tap "Connect WhatsApp" under "To go live".
5. Sign in to Facebook when Meta asks.
6. Choose your business portfolio.
7. Choose your WhatsApp Business account and number.
8. Allow the permissions it asks for.
9. Finish. Pull down on the WhatsApp screen to refresh.
10. Check that the top row says "Connected".

The Meta pages can be cramped on a phone. A computer is easier if you get stuck (verify the exact screens, Meta changes them).

## What clients notice

- Nothing, if you reconnect fast. Same number, same chat.
- While it is broken, they get no replies or reminders.
- Never delete your number in Meta to fix this. You can lose your templates and quality rating.

## Still stuck?

Send what you see on screen to whoever set up the app for you.
