**Why this matters:** Reminders cut no-shows. If they stop, you lose money without knowing it.

Check these in order. Stop when you find the problem.

## 1. Is WhatsApp connected?

1. Open Settings.
2. Tap WhatsApp.
3. Look at the top row. It should say "Connected" with your number.
4. If it says "Not connected" or "Reconnect", see "Reconnect WhatsApp".

## 2. Is a payment method added?

Reminders are business-started messages, so Meta needs a card on file. In Meta Business Suite, open WhatsApp Manager, then Payment methods, and add a card to the WhatsApp Business account. Do this on a computer.

## 3. Are the message templates approved?

1. Settings > WhatsApp.
2. In the Health section, find Message templates.
3. Tap "Show templates".
4. Each one should say Approved.
5. "In review" means wait for Meta.
6. "Rejected" or "Paused" means Meta stopped it. Send what you see on screen to whoever set up the app for you.

## 4. Is the number healthy?

1. In Settings > WhatsApp > Health, check Message quality and Message limit.
2. Red quality, or a number marked Flagged or Restricted, limits sending.
3. See "Number quality and messaging limits".

## 5. Can the client get the message?

- The number must be a real WhatsApp number in full international format.
- The client must have agreed to get messages from you.
- If the client blocked your business, Meta will not deliver.
- If the client's phone is off for a long time, Meta may drop the message.

## 6. Are reminder times set?

Reminder times are in the WhatsApp screen, not in Booking rules.

1. Settings > WhatsApp.
2. Scroll down to "Reminders to clients".
3. Tap it.
4. Choose one: 1 day and 2 hours, 1 day, 2 hours, or No reminders (verify the exact wording on screen).
5. If it says "No reminders", that is the reason.

Only the owner can change this.

## Is the booking too soon?

A reminder only sends if its time is still ahead. A booking made 1 hour ahead gets no "1 day" reminder.

## Still stuck?

Send what you see on screen to whoever set up the app for you.

Sources: app screens (WhatsApp settings, Reminders to clients, Health), Meta pricing docs.
