**Why this matters:** If Meta thinks clients dislike your messages, it lowers your quality and can cut how many clients you can message.

Find both in the app: Settings > WhatsApp > Health.

## Quality rating

The app shows Message quality as:

- **Green:** Clients are happy with your messages. (Meta calls this high.)
- **Yellow:** Some messages were blocked or reported. (Meta: medium.)
- **Red:** Meta may limit your number. (Meta: low.)

Meta works it out from the last 7 days. Newer days count more.

## What lowers quality

- Clients blocking or reporting your business.
- Clients muting or archiving your chat.
- Clients saying "didn't sign up" or "spam" when they block.
- Messages to people who did not ask for them.

Meta does not publish how many blocks it takes.

## Number status

Number status can show Connected, Pending, Flagged, Restricted or Disconnected.

When quality goes Red, Meta marks the number **Flagged**. While flagged, your limit cannot go up. If quality does not recover in 7 days, the status returns to Connected but your limit drops one level.

## Messaging limit tiers

This is how many different clients you can start a chat with in 24 hours.

- New businesses start at 250.
- Meta's docs list the next levels as 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and unlimited.
- The app may still show older names like "1,000 new conversations a day" (verify).
- Clients who message you first do not count.
- Limits rise on their own if your quality is good and you used at least half your limit in the last 7 days.
- Verifying your business can also raise it. See "Verify your business with Meta".

## How to recover

1. Stop sending anything clients did not ask for.
2. Send only reminders and confirmations for real bookings.
3. Make sure every client agreed to get messages.
4. Keep messages short and useful.
5. Wait. The rating updates as new days come in.
6. Pull down on Settings > WhatsApp to refresh.
