**Why this matters:** Reminders and confirmations are messages your business sends first. Meta charges for many of these messages. Without a payment method on file, Meta can stop delivering them.

**Do this on a computer.** Meta Business Suite is much easier on a big screen. On a phone it is hard to find the right page.

## Before you start

- Use a credit or debit card.
- Meta does not accept prepaid or virtual cards (verify).
- You need admin control of your Meta business portfolio.

## Steps

1. On a computer, open business.facebook.com and sign in.
2. Open Billing Hub. The direct page is business.facebook.com/latest/billing_hub/payment_methods/ (verify the menu name, it can change).
3. Choose your WhatsApp Business account if Meta asks which account to pay for.
4. Click "Add payment method" (verify the exact button text).
5. When Meta asks for a currency, choose **USD (US dollars)**.
6. Enter your card details.
7. Save.
8. Check that the card shows in the list for your WhatsApp Business account.

## Why USD

- Meta supports 16 billing currencies: USD, AED, ARS, AUD, BRL, CLP, COP, EUR, GBP, IDR, INR, MXN, MYR, PEN, SAR, SGD.
- ZAR (South African rand) is not on the list.
- Meta says you choose the currency when the account is created and it can't be changed after. Meta has a developer migration tool that moves you to a new account in another currency, so pick carefully.
- Your bank converts USD to rand. It may add a small fee.

## What Meta charges for

- Meta charges per delivered message your business sends, by message type and country.
- When a client messages you first, you can reply for free for the next 24 hours (service messages).
- Meta's pricing page says each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, and charges start after that from October 1, 2026 (verify the current rules).
- Clovie's plan price does not include Meta's message charges. Meta bills you directly.
