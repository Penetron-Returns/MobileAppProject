**Why this matters:** You get new bookings and messages that need a reply, right on your phone.

## Steps

1. Open Clovie.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Notifications.
4. Turn on "Notify me on this phone".
5. When Android asks, tap Allow.

## If it says "Turned off in your phone settings"

1. Open your phone's Settings app.
2. Tap Apps, then Clovie.
3. Tap Notifications.
4. Turn notifications on. (Menu names vary by phone brand.)
5. Go back to Clovie and check the switch again.

## Other checks

- Notifications need a real phone, not an emulator.
- Battery saver can block them. Allow Clovie to run in the background (verify the setting name on your phone).
- Notifications go to this phone only. Turn them on for each phone you use.
- Check that Do Not Disturb is off.
